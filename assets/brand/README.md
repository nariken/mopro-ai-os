# MOPRO AI OS brand assets

The **Living Stack** represents versioned Gadgets and packages rising through a controlled AI
runtime. Use the symbol on transparent backgrounds, the horizontal lockup for product headers, and
the app icon where a bounded square asset is required.

## Palette

- Kernel indigo: `#252B8F`
- Agent violet: `#7B6FE8`
- Context lavender: `#D8D2FF`
- Action vermilion: `#FF4F32`
- App-icon ivory: `#F7F4EA`

Keep the four layers, their order, and their flat fills intact. Do not add gradients, shadows, or
outlines. Preserve clear space around the mark equal to at least half the top tile's height.
